# PLAYTEST-0002: Revision 0.2 Owner Playtest Plan

## Status and purpose

This is a test plan, not a result. PLAYTEST-0002 has not yet occurred. Its purpose is to test whether
the Revision 0.2 candidate corrects the first playtest's comprehension, purpose, evidence, resource,
reward, summary, and second-Expedition failures while preserving the map's appeal.

## Clean start

Run the validated feature-branch build locally. Open developer details, choose **Reset local
prototype…**, confirm that only Long Map prototype history will be removed, and begin from the fresh
idle screen. Do not coach terminology during the first five minutes.

## First five minutes

Observe without intervention: first noticed element, time to explain the immediate objective, first
meaningful choice, whether two route cards are compared, which evidence and resource cues are used,
points of hesitation, scrolling, errors, and whether the player can return or recognize failure.

## Later tasks

Ask the player to:

1. choose a Commission and explain it;
2. compare two route cards and identify what is known or Unknown;
3. explain how a Report differs from current truth;
4. complete an Observation or salvage objective;
5. return to Lantern Harbor;
6. choose what to publish;
7. explain the Return and finalized previous-Expedition summaries;
8. spend earned Findings on preparation and begin a second Expedition;
9. trigger or inspect a failure if practical; and
10. reach and inspect Drift if practical.

## Questions

- Within 30 seconds, what is the objective and next choice?
- Is the map appealing and still the visual centerpiece?
- Is the Commission clear throughout the Expedition?
- Did route evidence change a decision? Why?
- What do Provisions, Charges, Return Reserve, and its projected margin mean?
- Why does Vessel Integrity matter, and what happens at zero?
- Were reward and publication timing visible?
- Did the summary clearly explain accomplishments, loss, Atlas change, and Findings?
- Is there a reason to begin a second Expedition?
- Did the compact region offer meaningful strategic depth?

## Revision 0.2 evidence gate

Pass only if the owner can explain the Commission within 30 seconds; compare historical route claims
without treating Unknown as safe or Reports as truth; describe Provision/Reserve and Integrity
consequences; complete the loop with no structural interface blocker; understand the summary and
reward; and identify a credible reason for a second Expedition. Fail or revise if purpose, evidence,
resources, failure, rewards, summary, or route depth remain materially unclear. Phase 1 stays open
until the evidence is recorded and reviewed.
